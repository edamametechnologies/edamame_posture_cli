## What's new in 2.2.0

### Detection
- Related detections from one program, for the same kind of activity in the same place, are grouped into a single finding that lists the events it covers (`episode` on findings).
- Improved attack detection. Fewer false alarms.

### Monitoring
- The detector status reports `recent_alertable_findings`: alertable findings first seen or seen again in the last 7 days.
- Findings that have not been seen again for 14 days may be marked stale (`stale: true`): they stay listed but no longer alert.
- The detector status reports where its process events come from (`process_events_source`).

### Other
- Security and stability improvements. Updating is recommended.
