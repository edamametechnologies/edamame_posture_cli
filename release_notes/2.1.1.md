## What's new in 2.1.1

### Detection
- Improved attack detection.
- Fewer false alarms when monitoring AI agents.

### Monitoring
- Sensor coverage reports a starting state while monitoring comes up after the daemon starts, instead of a degraded one.
- A detection's sessions stay available after the live capture has pruned them (`get_attack_pattern_finding_sessions`).
- When the AI analysis quota is used up, AI calls pause and resume on their own once the quota is back.

### Other
- Security and stability improvements. Updating is recommended.
